## Garden Description

In the nineteenth century, two houses were found in the course of creating the rue (street) Victor Hugo, which runs north from the railroad station. A strong retaining wall held up the terrace on which the house to the west was built. This house had two sectors. The garden was in the one on the south. The fragmentary ruins show that it was built around a peristyle [1] with three porticoes [2-3-4] and ornamented with a rectangular pool, later transformed into a U-shaped channel pool. On the axis of the garden there opened to the east a big reception room [5].
